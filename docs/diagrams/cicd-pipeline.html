CI/CD — two pipelines, different gates

Internal services and the internal React app share one pipeline with standard peer review. Anything that touches client-facing behavior or live trading logic — the partner portal, third-party FIX/REST endpoints, or a strategy version promotion — runs on a second pipeline with an extra compliance/product gate.
